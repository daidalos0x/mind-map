# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming a mistake was intentional.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and derogatory comments.
- Harassment in public or private, including unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, in issue and pull request discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer, [@phaethix](https://github.com/phaethix), through GitHub. If the report is sensitive, use the private advisory form at <https://github.com/phaethix/mind-map/security/advisories/new> and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are read by the maintainer alone.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review and investigate every report, and will respond in a way that is appropriate to the circumstances. Consequences escalate with severity and repetition:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was wrong and why.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including a temporary pause on interaction.
3. **Temporary ban** — a time-limited ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any interaction with the project.
